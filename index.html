Constitution, Regulations & Guidelines

Rivora Arts & Sports Club functions in accordance with democratic values, non-discrimination, and dedication to grassroots athletics.

  • Universal voting rights apply to all approved members regardless of subscription clearance.
  • Subscriptions are categorized into regular monthly tiers (₹150 for active regulars / ₹30 for general contributors).
  • Executive authority is vested in elected leadership, managed transparently with strict audit accountability.
